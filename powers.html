Overview

Sung Jinwoo possesses a variety of powers that make him one of the most formidable characters in his universe. Below are some of his key abilities:

Shadow Extraction

This ability allows Jinwoo to extract shadows from defeated enemies and turn them into allies.

Enhanced Strength

Jinwoo's physical strength is significantly enhanced, allowing him to perform feats beyond normal human capabilities.

Regeneration

He has the ability to heal rapidly from injuries, making him difficult to defeat in battle.

Skill Acquisition

Jinwoo can learn and acquire skills from others, making him adaptable in various combat situations.
